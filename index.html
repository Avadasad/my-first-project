Help the street dogs

Hi! We are two dog volunteers. My wife is from Odesa, Ukraine. When the war began we had to leave, and now we live in Georgia. We cannot go back.

We have always loved dogs. Morgan, our German Shepherd, lived a long life. Tyson, our Staffordshire, left us too soon. Because of all the moving we cannot have a dog of our own right now, so we feed and look after the street dogs around us.

We also send money to volunteer groups in Ukraine that rescue animals from the shelling, run shelters and find them new homes.

Sneaky Yard is our way to show how many obstacles a street dog meets. If you enjoy it and want to help, thank you. Donations are voluntary. Part of the money goes to the dogs: food, medicine and rescue. The rest keeps this game alive and growing.
