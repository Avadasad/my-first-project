Help the street dogs

Hi! We are volunteers who help dogs. We have always loved dogs. We look after the street dogs living near us: we feed them and take them to the vet. My wife is from Odesa, and we had to leave Ukraine when the war began. Now we live in Georgia.

We also send money to volunteer groups in Ukraine that rescue animals from shelling zones, run shelters and find new homes for them.

Sneaky Yard is more than a game. It is our way to show what street dogs go through. I made it myself, and if you enjoy it and want to help, we would be very grateful. Donations are voluntary. Part of the money goes straight to the dogs: food, medicine and rescue. The rest helps keep this game running and growing.
